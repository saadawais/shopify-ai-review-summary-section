# ⭐ Shopify Amazon-Style AI Review Summary Section (OS 2.0)

A lightweight Shopify Online Store 2.0 section inspired by Amazon's AI-generated customer review takeaways. Summarizes key sentiment, strengths, and sizing feedback directly on the product page.

---

## 🎯 Conversion Value
- **Instant Social Proof:** Gives shoppers an immediate 5-second summary of customer feedback without making them scroll through dozens of reviews.
- **Reduces Returns:** Clear feedback badges (e.g., *"Runs Slightly Slim"*) set customer expectations upfront.
- **Zero App Fees:** Built directly into the theme schema without requiring expensive monthly review-widget add-ons.

---

## 🛠 Installation
1. Add `sections/ai-review-summary.liquid` to your theme's `/sections` folder.
2. In the Shopify Customizer on any **Product Template**, click **Add section** $\rightarrow$ **AI Review Highlights**.
